About the App

What Social Cleaner Does

Social Cleaner is a powerful, privacy-first storage management tool that helps you keep your Android device clean and organized — without ever compromising your personal data.

WhatsApp Media Manager

Browse, preview, and selectively delete WhatsApp photos, videos, audio, and documents directly from the app.

Duplicate File Cleaner

Smart scanning detects identical files across your storage so you can reclaim wasted space with confidence.

Status Viewer & Saver

View and save WhatsApp statuses before they disappear — privately, without notifying the sender.

Junk File Remover

Identify and remove residual files, temporary caches, and leftover app data to keep your device running fast.

Smart Storage Organizer

Gain a clear visual overview of how your storage is used and organize media into easy-to-manage categories.

Privacy-First Design

All operations happen entirely on your device. No files are uploaded, no personal data is collected or sold.

Permissions

Why We Request Permissions

We only request permissions that are strictly necessary for the app to function. Below is a transparent breakdown of each permission and exactly what it is — and is not — used for.

All Files Access
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

This permission enables Social Cleaner to scan and manage your device's storage — the core functionality of the app. Without it, the app cannot detect duplicate files, WhatsApp media, or junk files.

✅ This permission is used for:

  • Scanning media files on your device storage
  • Managing and organizing WhatsApp media
  • Detecting and removing duplicate files
  • Identifying and cleaning junk & residual files

❌ We will never:

  • Upload your personal documents or photos
  • Access private files beyond the app's stated purpose
  • Collect or transmit file content to external servers
Internet Access
INTERNET

Internet access is required to load advertisements (which keep the app free), send anonymous usage analytics, and check for app updates. No personal file data is ever transmitted.

✅ Used strictly for:

  • Loading banner and rewarded advertisements
  • Anonymous crash reporting & analytics
  • Fetching app configuration updates
  • No personal files are transmitted over the internet
  • No user identity is linked to analytics
Ad Network Access
AD_ID · NETWORK_STATE

Social Cleaner uses advertising SDKs (AdMob and AppLovin) to display ads that support the app's free availability. These SDKs may collect an anonymous advertising identifier for ad personalization.

✅ Ad types served:

  • Banner ads (non-intrusive display ads)
  • Rewarded video ads (optional, for user benefits)
  • Interstitial ads (shown between interactions)
  • No sensitive personal data is used for ad targeting
  • Ad ID can be reset anytime in Android settings

Data Safety

Your Data, Your Control

Social Cleaner is built with a local-first, privacy-by-design philosophy. Your files never leave your device, and no personal data is collected, stored, or sold.

Files Stay On-Device
All scanning, sorting, and deletion operations happen entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded to any server.
No Unauthorized Upload
The app does not have functionality to upload your photos, videos, documents, or any other personal files to external servers.
No Data Selling
We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data to third parties, ever. Your data is yours, and we treat it that way.
Secure by Design
The app only accesses storage-related media that is directly necessary for its stated cleaning and organizing functions.
User-Controlled Actions
Every file deletion and cleanup action requires explicit approval from you. Nothing is removed automatically without your confirmation.
No Account Required
Social Cleaner works completely without creating an account. We don't collect email addresses, names, or any identifying information.
User Controls

You Are Always In Control

Social Cleaner respects your autonomy. Here is exactly how you can manage, limit, or remove the app's access at any time.

1
Revoke Permissions Anytime Go to Android Settings → Apps → Social Cleaner → Permissions. You can revoke any permission at any time without uninstalling the app.
2
Control Media Cleanup Actions Every deletion in Social Cleaner requires your explicit confirmation. Scan results are shown first — you choose what to delete, one file or one batch at a time.
3
Reset Your Advertising ID Go to Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID. This creates a new anonymous ID, disconnecting your history from future ad targeting.
4
Opt Out of Personalized Ads Go to Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization. The app will still show ads, but they will not be personalized to your interests.
5
Uninstall the App Completely You can uninstall Social Cleaner at any time from your device settings. Uninstalling removes all app data from your device immediately.
