1. Who we are

"Media Cleaner", "we" and "us" refer to the independent developer of the Media Cleaner: Free Up Storage Android app (package com.bhargav.mediacleaner), published on Google Play. You can reach us at the email address in section 14.

2. What the app does

  • Chat media cleaner — finds photos, videos, voice notes, audio, documents, GIFs, stickers and saved statuses that WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Instagram and Telegram store in shared storage, sorts them into categories and lets you preview and delete the ones you choose.
  • Junk Cleaner — finds this app's own cache, thumbnail caches, temporary and log files, unfinished downloads, installer (APK) files and empty folders, and removes only the items you leave ticked.
  • Storage overview — shows free and used space on your phone.
  • Other social apps — shows which popular social apps from a fixed list are installed and, if you allow Usage access, how much storage each one uses, with a shortcut to that app's system settings.

3. Information handled only on your device

To work, the app reads information on your phone. None of the items below is sent to us or to any third party.

  • File details — names, sizes, types, dates and folder locations of media and junk files, used to list them and to delete the ones you pick. The app does not read the content of your documents; images and videos are only decoded to show previews on screen.
  • Scan summary — category sizes and a few file references used for previews are saved in the app's private storage so the home screen opens instantly. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Installed social apps and their storage size — the app checks only a fixed list of social apps (for example Snapchat, Facebook or YouTube). It does not read the full list of apps on your phone.
  • Installer file details — for APK files the Junk Cleaner reads the app name and version stored inside the file so you can recognise it.
  • Settings — your theme choice and whether you finished the introduction screens.

6. How information is used

  • To provide the cleaning features on your device.
  • To show ads that keep the app free, and to limit how often they appear.
  • To understand usage, fix crashes and improve the app, using the services in section 4.

We don't use your files or file details for advertising, and we don't build profiles from them.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the Google services listed in section 4, for the purposes described there, or when required by law.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data kept on your phone by the app is deleted when you uninstall it or clear its data in system Settings.
  • Files you delete with the app are removed from your phone permanently. Media Cleaner has no copy of them and cannot restore them.
  • Firebase and AdMob keep data according to Google's retention settings. For example, Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days, and Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.
  • We have no account system, so we hold no personal profile about you. To ask for deletion of analytics data linked to your app installation, email us.

9. Security

The cleaning features run entirely on your device. The Google services above send data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we choose well-established providers and collect as little as possible.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Ad consent — if you are in a region that requires it, you'll be asked for consent on first launch and can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the app.
  • Personalised ads — delete or reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads. You'll still see ads, but they won't be personalised.
  • Permissions — turn any of them off in Android Settings at any time.
  • Your rights — depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, object to or restrict processing, and complain to a data protection authority. Email us and we'll help.

11. Children

Media Cleaner is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help remove it.
